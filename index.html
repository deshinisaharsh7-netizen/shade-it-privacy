Shade It! Privacy Policy

Comfort your view.

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Shade It! is a Microsoft Edge extension that lets users darken, invert, or dim selected reading regions on webpages and supported PDF documents. We designed it with privacy in mind.

1. Information We Collect

Shade It! does not operate servers and does not directly collect or transmit personal information to us. It stores website hostnames, reading-region coordinates and dimensions, display preferences, and enabled status locally in your browser. Local PDF profiles may contain file paths to restore settings.

2. Website and PDF Access

The extension requires access to webpages and supported PDFs to display the region-selection interface, apply visual effects, and restore saved settings. Page content is not uploaded to Shade It! servers.

3. Local Storage and Data Control

Settings are stored in your browser using chrome.storage.local. They are not stored on our servers or synchronized by Shade It!. You can reset saved profiles through the extension. Removing the extension removes its stored data according to browser behavior.

4. Analytics and Tracking

Shade It! does not use analytics, advertising trackers, telemetry, tracking cookies, accounts, or background data collection. We do not track browsing activity.

5. Feedback

If you choose to submit feedback, the extension opens an external Google Forms page. Information you voluntarily submit is processed by Google Forms and may be accessible to the form owner. Feedback is optional. The extension does not automatically attach browsing information, saved profiles, or page contents to feedback submissions.

6. Third-Party Services

The extension does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising. The optional feedback form is hosted by Google Forms and subject to Google's privacy practices.

7. Children's Privacy

Shade It! is a general-purpose reading utility and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Personal information is not required to use its core functionality.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's features or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
